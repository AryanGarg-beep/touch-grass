How it works

Getting notes in

  • Import from recorder: plug the Pico in by USB. Its notes become one outing, then get transcribed and written up. Tick Clear the recorder afterwards to empty it.
  • Import a saved recorder folder: a folder you copied off the recorder. Include its index.csv to keep the timings.
  • Add phone recordings: any audio files; together they become one outing.

Reading an outing

  • Times are minutes after setting out. ≈ means estimated: the recorder restarted, or a note had no line in index.csv.
  • Click a transcript to fix a misheard word, then press Rewrite entry.
  • Merge into the outing above joins two outings that were really one walk.

Weather

  • Set a PIN code (then pick your area) or a city in Settings. The banner needs SERPAPI_KEY in fieldnotes/.env.
  • Only your area goes to SerpApi, at most once an hour; a PIN code goes to India Post to find its areas. Gemma decides whether it's worth going out.

Whisper models

  • tiny.en is fastest, base.en is a good default, small.en is more accurate but slower. Models without .en understand any language.
